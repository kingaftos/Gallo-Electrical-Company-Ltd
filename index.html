About Gallo Electrical

Established in 1991 and registered under the Corporate Affairs Commission of the Federal Republic of Nigeria (RC: 1026032), Gallo Electrical Company Limited is an indigenous firm with deep experience in electrical services across the country.

We specialise in the complete installation of electrical services and civil works for commercial, industrial and public developments. Our work ranges from routine maintenance to large-scale projects worth hundreds of millions of naira.

We believe in building strong, lasting relationships with our clients — delivering high-quality work on time, within budget, and to a standard that earns repeat business.

  • Certified Staffs Dedicated engineers and technicians
  • Major Clients Oando, Ardova, Conoil, ENYO Retails, Pinnacle Oil
  • Nationwide Reach Established bases in Lagos and Ogun State, certified engineers serving all 36 states
  • Our Commitment Quality, Health & Safety, Training and Environmental care

Our Capabilities

Four core areas of electrical engineering expertise

01

Power Systems

Complete design and installation of electrical power infrastructure.

  • High-voltage transformer installations (50kVA–500kVA and above)
  • Low-voltage distribution systems and switchboards
  • Power line design and installation
  • Earthing, bonding and lightning protection
  • Standby generation and UPS systems
02

Automation & Controls

Advanced electrical control and automation solutions.

  • Bespoke switchgear manufacture and installation
  • Load balancing and distribution control
  • Security lighting and surveillance systems
  • Automated fault detection and management
03

MEP Engineering

Full mechanical, electrical and plumbing coordination for buildings.

  • Integrated MEP design for commercial and industrial buildings
  • Building automation systems
  • Energy-efficient lighting solutions
  • Complete building infrastructure installations
04

Field Maintenance

Ongoing support and maintenance to keep your systems running.

  • Routine electrical maintenance and inspections
  • Equipment testing (insulation, earth fault, voltage)
  • Emergency repairs and troubleshooting
  • Preventive maintenance programmes
